# Orders

An order contains the details of food selected by a customer.

## Order Information

- Order ID
- Customer ID
- Restaurant ID
- Selected food items
- Quantity
- Total amount
- Order status

## Order Process

1. Customer selects food.
2. Customer places the order.
3. Restaurant accepts the order.
4. Food is prepared.
5. Delivery partner collects the order.
6. Order is delivered to the customer.
