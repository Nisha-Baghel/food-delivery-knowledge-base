# Business Rules

1. Only registered customers can place orders.

2. A restaurant must be open to accept an order.

3. An order is confirmed after successful payment.

4. A delivery partner is assigned after the order is confirmed.

5. The restaurant prepares the order after accepting it.

6. Customers can track their orders after a delivery partner is assigned.

7. An order can be cancelled before food preparation.

8. A cancellation charge may apply after food preparation.
