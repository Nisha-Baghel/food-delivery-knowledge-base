# Food Delivery Knowledge Repository

## Introduction

This repository contains structured knowledge about an online food delivery business system.

## Purpose

The purpose of this repository is to organize important business information, rules, and processes in one place.

## Contents

- Customer information
- Restaurant information
- Order management
- Payment process
- Delivery process
- Business rules
- Cancellation policy

## Main Business Process

Customer
   ↓
Restaurant Selection
   ↓
Food Selection
   ↓
Order Placement
   ↓
Payment
   ↓
Restaurant Preparation
   ↓
Delivery
   ↓
Order Completed

## Conclusion

This repository provides a structured representation of the knowledge required for an online food delivery business system.
