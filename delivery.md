# Delivery

Delivery is the process of transporting the prepared order from the restaurant to the customer.

## Delivery Process

1. Delivery partner is assigned.
2. Partner collects the order from the restaurant.
3. Order is transported to the customer's address.
4. Order is delivered.
5. Delivery status is updated.

## Delivery Status

- Assigned
- Picked up
- Out for delivery
- Delivered
